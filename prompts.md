# SEO MCP Server: prompts to copy

Paste these into Claude, Claude Code or Cursor once the server is set up with `SEMUST_MCP_TOOLSETS=gsc,rank,aiRank,indexing,changes,competitor`. If you have several Semust projects, name the one you mean, or the assistant will ask. Dates are worked out for you; say "last 30 days" or "last month".

## Quick wins

- List my 20 keywords in positions 11–20 with the most impressions, and the page that ranks for each.
- Which 10 pages could gain the most clicks from a better title? Suggest a new title for each.
- Find long-tail queries of four or more words that already bring me impressions.

## Traffic drops and content decay

- Which 10 pages lost the most clicks over the last 6 months, and when did each decline start?
- Why did my organic clicks drop last week? Show the site changes and ranking moves from the same days.
- Compare the last 60 days with the 60 before: which queries gained and which lost the most clicks?
- What changed on the site in the week before traffic dropped? Group the changes by page.

## Cannibalization and content audit

- Do I have pages competing with each other for the same query? Recommend which one should stay.
- List my lowest-traffic pages that could be merged into others.
- Build an FAQ section from the how and why questions that bring people to my site.

## Rankings and AI Overviews

- Give me my ranking overview: visibility score, average position and yesterday's changes.
- Which keywords lost the most positions in the last 7 days on mobile?
- For how many tracked queries am I cited in an AI Overview, and which domains are cited instead of me?

## Indexing

- What is my indexing health score, and how many pages are not indexed?
- Has my number of non-indexed pages grown in the last 30 days?

## Reporting and competitors

- Prepare my monthly SEO report for last month, with month-over-month and year-over-year changes.
- Show my latest competitor analysis: which keywords do my competitors rank for that I don't?
- What would a new competitor analysis cost, and do I have the credits?
